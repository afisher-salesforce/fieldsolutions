Connected Customer Vision | Siemens Mobility Field Service Journey

Extend the current Service Cloud foundation with purpose-built field capabilities to eliminate spreadsheet operations, recover technician capacity, and increase warranty confidence.

Brand: #009999 Audience: Siemens Mobility + Salesforce Format: Executive Meeting Narrative

Executive Summary

Strategic framing for a phased, additive Salesforce expansion aligned to field operations outcomes.

Executive Context

Siemens Mobility is scaling field operations across multiple sites while running safety-critical delivery through workflows never designed for this complexity. The strategy is additive: preserve current Salesforce investment and activate missing capabilities around it.

Executive Stakes by Persona

  • Field Service Leadership: recover productive technician time and improve first-time fix consistency.
  • Operations and Logistics Leadership: establish auditable part movement and RMA traceability.
  • Customer and Account Leadership: provide transparent warranty experience and strengthen renewal posture.
  • IT and Platform Leadership: scale with configuration-first architecture instead of point-solution sprawl.

Transformation Outcomes

  • Administrative drag reduced through workflow automation and mobile-first execution.
  • Asset intelligence standardized across vehicles, components, revisions, and vendor loops.
  • Field and logistics lifecycle conflict removed through linked but independent process records.
  • Customer communication shifted from manual status requests to structured visibility.
