Siemens Mobility | Connected Field Vision

Executive discussion site for evolving Siemens Mobility's current Service Cloud foundation into a connected field operating model that removes spreadsheet-dependent workflows, increases technician productivity, and strengthens warranty relationships with transit customers.

Connected Customer Vision

Preserve Siemens Mobility's existing Salesforce investment while extending it with purpose-built capabilities for field execution, asset lifecycle control, logistics coordination, and customer transparency.

Executive Framing

This is an additive transformation. The current case framework remains the foundation while Salesforce capabilities are configured to resolve workflow conflicts, recover technician capacity, and create an auditable field operations record that scales as new sites open.

Transformation Outcomes

  • Recover 2-3 productive technician hours per day through workflow automation.
  • Replace orphaned asset and part records with complete lifecycle traceability.
  • Separate field completion and logistics/RMA workflows to end case reopening cycles.
  • Move from CSV and email updates to real-time customer visibility through portal patterns.
  • Deliver operational dashboards that quantify value and support budget decisions.

Recommended Meeting CTA

Align on a 90-minute working session to map Siemens Mobility's current implementation to a phased rollout plan: Phase 1 productivity and process clarity, Phase 2 customer visibility and analytics, Phase 3 AI-assisted field operations.

Vignette 1 | The Half-Day Tax

Shift technicians from administrative overhead to field execution by orchestrating work order, asset, and inventory workflows in a mobile experience.

Current-State Friction

  • Technicians spend roughly half the day on manual updates and spreadsheet lookups.
  • Inventory confidence is low because system values and physical reality drift apart.
  • Field completion is slowed by manual date/status entry and duplicate communication.

Salesforce Capability Activation

FWO: Field Service Work Orders Structured field lifecycle and mobile execution.
FMO: Field Worker Mobility Asset context, guided steps, and completion capture on mobile.
FIR: Field Inventory & Replenishment Real-time parts consumption and stock accuracy.
FSM: Operational Schedule Management Optimized assignment based on skill/location.

Executive Value Statement

Even a conservative productivity recovery creates the equivalent of adding field capacity without additional hiring while improving first response and resolution speed.

Vignette 2 | The Asset That Nobody Owns

Replace fragmented asset and RMA tracking with one auditable record for every component movement, revision, and vendor handoff.

Current-State Friction

  • Parts move through vendor loops without consistent status and accountability.
  • Revision level history and field modifications are hard to reconstruct.
  • Lost-part risk and warranty exposure increase when records are incomplete.

Salesforce Capability Activation

IMP: Asset Lifecycle Management Hierarchy from vehicle to sub-component.
FDR: RMA / Depot Repair Stage-driven vendor workflow and SLA checkpoints.
FPM: Install Base & Preventative Maintenance Service history and revision continuity.
VWA: Warranty Management Warranty evidence, claims traceability, and dispute readiness.

Executive Value Statement

Asset intelligence becomes a strategic control point: better vendor accountability, faster audits, and lower warranty liability across an expanding fleet.

Vignette 3 | The Case in Conflict

End field-versus-logistics collisions by separating workflows into linked records with independent definitions of done.

Current-State Friction

  • Field teams need case closure at customer completion; logistics needs continued tracking.
  • Records are repeatedly reopened, obscuring true operational status.
  • Related-case workarounds create complexity and inconsistent visibility.

Salesforce Capability Activation

VCM: Incident Management Separate work streams per operational objective.
VTM: Case Teaming Role-specific views with shared parent context.
FDR: RMA / Depot Repair Independent lifecycle for vendor loop completion.
VQL: Service Quality Analytics Distinct KPIs for field close and logistics throughput.

Executive Value Statement

Process clarity removes structural conflict, improves cycle times, and gives leadership a reliable picture of field completion versus downstream logistics status.

Vignette 4 | The Customer Who Doesn't Know

Convert manual CSV intake and email follow-up into real-time customer visibility and structured digital collaboration.

Current-State Friction

  • Customer requests require export/import and manual queue creation.
  • Status communication depends on ad-hoc email responses.
  • Warranty confidence erodes when updates are delayed or incomplete.

Salesforce Capability Activation

VSS: Experience Cloud / Self-Service Customer portal for status and request submission.
VEN: Service Contract & Entitlements Warranty SLA alignment and obligations tracking.
VCH: Omni-Channel + Notification Flows Proactive, structured communication at status changes.
FAP / ABI: Operational Dashboards QBR-ready fleet health and at-risk repair visibility.

Executive Value Statement

Transparency changes the commercial dynamic from reactive status chasing to trusted partnership, supporting renewals and expansion.

Vignette 5 | The Connected Field

Unify technicians, logistics, management, and customers on a single operating model that preserves existing investment and scales without disruption.

Operating Model Principles

  • Additive architecture: existing case framework and history remain intact.
  • Scalable design: new sites inherit proven workflows and controls.
  • Evidence-based management: performance dashboards replace spreadsheet rollups.
  • AI is phase-two acceleration after operational foundations are in place.

Salesforce Capability Activation

Platform Consolidation Unified data model across service, field, asset, and customer domains.
ABI / FAP: Analytics & Reporting Site, technician, and warranty performance visibility.
DAG: Agentforce (optional next phase) Summaries and recommendations once core workflows stabilize.

Executive Value Statement

The spreadsheet ends and the platform begins: Siemens Mobility gains a field operations system that can grow with demand and sustain service quality at enterprise scale.
