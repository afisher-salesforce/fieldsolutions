Salesforce Capability Domains

Domain-level view of the Salesforce capabilities that power the Siemens Mobility field transformation, including what each domain contributes to operational outcomes.

Field Service Domain

Field Service capabilities operationalize work execution, asset workflows, and part movement across technicians and sites.

FWO: Work Order ManagementDefines the lifecycle for service work from intake through completion.
FMO: Field Worker MobilityProvides mobile-first technician experiences with in-context operational data.
FIR: Field Inventory & Replenishment ManagementTracks part availability, consumption, and replenishment in near real time.
FDR: RMA / Depot RepairGoverns vendor return, repair, and handback workflows with status accountability.
FAP: Field Service Analysis & PlanningSupports performance reporting and planning across sites and teams.
FSM: Operational Schedule ManagementOptimizes dispatch and assignment against skill, location, and priority.

Service Domain

Service capabilities govern case lifecycle, customer obligations, communication channels, and service quality measurement.

VCM: Case ManagementManages service request intake, ownership, and resolution traceability.
VEN: Service Contract Management & EntitlementAligns service execution to warranty and SLA commitments.
VSS: Self ServiceProvides customer-facing visibility and request channels through portal patterns.
VCH: Omni-Channel Contact CenterOrchestrates customer interactions consistently across communication channels.
VTM: Case TeamingSupports cross-functional collaboration without collapsing role ownership.
VQL: Service Quality & Supervision AnalyticsMeasures operational quality and team performance across service delivery.
VWA: Warranty ManagementTracks warranty evidence and claim adjudication for compliance and dispute readiness.

Analytics & Reporting Domain

Analytics capabilities provide executive visibility, operational governance, and early risk identification for proactive decisions.

ABI: Embedded BI & DashboardsSurfaces KPI-driven insight directly in operational workflows and leadership views.
APC: Tableau Pulse / Proactive InsightsPushes anomaly and trend signals to leaders without manual report polling.
ARI: Revenue IntelligenceLinks performance and pipeline quality indicators to commercial decision-making.

Data & AI Domain

Data and AI capabilities create trusted intelligence layers that support prediction, summarization, and decision acceleration.

DAG: Agentic AI / Autonomous AgentsEnables AI-driven support flows for summaries, recommendations, and guided actions.
DPA: Predictive AI / Einstein ScoringApplies scoring models to forecast operational risk and service outcomes.
DEL: Einstein Trust LayerProvides AI governance controls for safety, auditability, and policy compliance.
DVS: Vector Search & RAG GroundingGrounds generated responses using enterprise content and service knowledge sources.

Platform & Governance Domain

Platform governance capabilities ensure secure scale, policy alignment, and sustainable operation as rollout expands.

PGV: Data Governance & PrivacyApplies access, privacy, and compliance controls to operational data usage.
PMD: Multi-Org / Subsidiary ArchitectureSupports scalable enterprise deployment patterns across business units and geographies.
