Salesforce Capability Domains

Domain-level view of the Salesforce capabilities that power the Siemens Mobility field transformation, including what each domain contributes to operational outcomes.

Capability Framework in Business Context

This view presents Salesforce capability breadth in practical terms for Siemens Mobility: what each domain does, which capabilities are most relevant now, and how adjacent licensed capabilities can be activated over time.

Field Service

FWO: Work Order Management — governs execution lifecycle from dispatch to close.

FMO: Field Worker Mobility — mobile-first technician workflows with in-context data.

FIR: Field Inventory & Replenishment Management — controls part availability and consumption integrity.

FDR: RMA / Depot Repair — structures vendor-loop repair and return workflows.

FPM: Install Base & Preventative Maintenance — maintains component history and preventive cadence.

FSM: Operational Schedule Management — optimizes assignments by skill, location, and priority.

Service Operations

VCM: Case Management — tracks service request ownership and resolution traceability.

VEN: Service Contract Management & Entitlement — enforces warranty and SLA obligations.

VSS: Self Service — extends customer visibility and request intake through portal patterns.

VCH: Omni-Channel Contact Center — unifies communication channels with governed routing.

VTM: Case Teaming — supports cross-functional collaboration without ownership ambiguity.

VQL: Service Quality & Supervision Analytics — monitors service quality and team performance.

VWA: Warranty Management — strengthens warranty evidence, claims, and dispute readiness.

Analytics & Reporting

ABI: Embedded BI & Dashboards — delivers KPI visibility within operational workflows.

APC: Tableau Pulse / Proactive Insights — pushes anomaly alerts to leadership proactively.

ARI: Revenue Intelligence — links commercial health signals to operational performance.

AEX: Einstein Conversation Insights — mines interaction quality and coaching insights.

Data & AI

DAG: Agentic AI / Autonomous Agents — enables next-step recommendations and autonomous task support.

DPA: Predictive AI / Einstein Scoring — predicts risk, delays, and service outcomes.

DEL: Einstein Trust Layer — governs AI safety, privacy, and audit controls.

DVS: Vector Search & RAG Grounding — grounds AI outputs in trusted enterprise content.

DIR / DUP: Identity Resolution + Unified Profiles — unifies fragmented customer and asset context.

DDH: Data Harmonization — maps external operational data to a standard model.

Integration

IEI: Enterprise Integration — orchestrates process flows across Salesforce and core systems.

IEP: Enterprise Platform Connectors — accelerates integration with packaged connectors.

IAM: API Management — secures, governs, and scales enterprise API usage.

IAF: Agent-to-Agent Orchestration — coordinates specialized agents for cross-domain workflows.

IMS: Microsoft Ecosystem Integration — aligns collaboration and CRM actions across Microsoft tooling.

Platform & Governance

PGV: Data Governance & Privacy — enforces policy, access, and compliance controls.

PMD: Multi-Org / Subsidiary Architecture — supports enterprise scaling patterns.

PNZ: Net Zero / Sustainability Management — extends reporting for ESG and sustainability governance.

Revenue, Order, and Commercial Ops

OIM / ORM: Inventory + Order Management — aligns product, fulfillment, and availability workflows.

CCM / CQM / CPM: CPQ Core — controls catalog, quoting, and pricing consistency.

BLL / BIM / BCC: Billing Foundation — supports invoice, billing, and collections lifecycle.

SOP / SAM / SCL: Sales Core — aligns opportunity execution, account strategy, and contract governance.
